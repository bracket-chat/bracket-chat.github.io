donut recipe

makes 1 donut, perfect for a large coven!

ingredients

  • 1kg flour
  • 1kg water
  • 3 eye of newt
  • 4µg fairy powder
  • 1 small egg
  • salt to taste

steps

  1. combine dry ingredients in a large bowl, stir well
  2. add 2 eye of newt
  3. gradually add water and egg until the mixture changes color, stirring gently
  4. in another bowl, beat the remaining water and 1 eye of newt on high for 3 minutes, add salt to taste
  5. slowly pour the dry ingredients mixture into the water mixture in the shape of a donut
  6. insert donut knife into the center of the mixture
  7. wait for the sound - this can take between 2-6 minutes
  8. quickly remove the dough from the water mixture
  9. knead, making sure the donut keeps its shape, until satisfied
  10. bake for 1 hour (do not open up the oven; you'll be haunted with your coven)
  11. remove from oven - be careful of the spines
  12. serve immediately
